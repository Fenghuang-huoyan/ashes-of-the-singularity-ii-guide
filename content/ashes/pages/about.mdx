This is a short English wiki for **Ashes of the Singularity II**, Steam AppID **3499790**, built as a static site for **ashesofthesingularityii.wiki**.

## Disclaimer

ashesofthesingularityii.wiki is an unofficial fan-made guide for *Ashes of the Singularity II*. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Oxide Games, Stardock Entertainment, Valve, or Steam**. All game content, names, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. This site does not sell the game, process purchases, or recover accounts. Not responsible for purchase decisions or for balance changes after a pre-release post.

Nothing here is account support. The Steam store page lists https://support.stardock.com/ and does not list an email. That site is Stardock’s, not this wiki’s.

The [guide](/guide) and [factions](/factions) pages quote dated official text. A health or damage figure appears only when a Stardock post printed it, and it is labeled with that date. There is no codes page because those sources do not publish a redeem program.

<Callout kind="note" title="What “checked” means">
“Checked 6 Nov 2026” means the Steam API, the English store page, and the linked official posts were read that day. It does not mean someone played the launch build. The game was still marked coming soon.
</Callout>

## Images

Hero, capsules, the store header, and screenshots are official Steam store assets for AppID 3499790, downloaded from the Steam CDN and served from this site so pages do not hotlink Steam. The unhashed `library_header.jpg` URL returned 404 that day and is not in the set. The file list and source URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/steam/ATTRIBUTION.txt).

## Privacy

The site is static. It has no accounts and does not ask for your Steam login or a Stardock login.

GA4 `G-CJ1920QPM9` is set in `analytics.gaId`. The base layout loads that tag on content pages.

The Adsterra Native Banner slot named `afterQuickAnswer` is enabled with this site's own Get Code. Zone id `e0abcfd137a30161b563f15f410971d2` loads from `https://bauval.org/21/e0abcfd137a30161b563f15f410971d2` on the homepage and guide pages. No advertising script from another game is included. Adult and sensitive categories stay off.

The same privacy note is in the footer of every page, under the heading Privacy. The disclaimer paragraph in that footer is the short form of this page.

## What is still unpublished

- A numeric Steam price. The 14 October 2026 announcement mentions a launch discount and does not print it. The 6 November appdetails payload had an empty package list.
- A full UEF and Substrate stat roster confirmed in the launch client.
- Whether the 16 February 2026 PHC health and damage figures survive balance passes.
- Per-second income for Turinium, Elerite, or the region baseline.
